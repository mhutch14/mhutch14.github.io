Company holidays

Holidays inside a week are filled in automatically as 8 paid hours. Floatable holidays can be banked: work ≥ 44 paid hours (holiday included) in that week and the holiday is banked automatically, costing 48 − hours of PTO. You can also tick them manually.

Profile

A 30-minute lunch is deducted automatically when your day spans it (unless WTL is on). Years of service decide your PTO accrual.

Your data

Everything is saved automatically in this browser, and to your account when you're signed in (top of the page) so it follows you between devices. Export a backup now and then.

How it works

  • Hours are counted in 6-minute units (tenths of an hour) using the same rounding as the original workbook, so the totals line up with your time system.
  • Clock in stamps your IN time and keeps OUT = now every second. Stop freezes OUT. Resuming after a gap offers to log it as a break.
  • Lunch: 30 minutes from your lunch start are deducted when you work across it. Tick WTL to skip the deduction for a day.
  • Breaks (clocked out and back in) are subtracted from that day's paid hours. Don't log your normal lunch — it's automatic.
  • New week (Sunday): last week is archived and cleared. If it's under 40 hours you're asked whether to cover the gap with PTO.
  • PTO accrues on the Friday ending the first full week of each month; you're asked before it's added, capped at 1.5× your annual accrual.
  • Even clock out is the earliest you can leave and still be on pace for 8 hours a day this week.
